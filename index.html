Stock Market Glossary for Beginners

P/E Ratio (Price to Earnings) Measures a company's current share price relative to its earnings per share. A higher P/E often indicates expected high futures growth.
Market Capitalization The total market value of a company's outstanding shares of stock (Share Price x Total Number of Shares).
52-Week Range The lowest and highest price at which a stock has traded over the past 52 weeks (1 year).
